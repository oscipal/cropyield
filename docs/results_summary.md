# Results summary

Generated by `scripts/LSTM/10_summary.py`. All numbers are **test field R²** (mean prediction vs. mean yield per field-year); for ensembles the prediction is the mean of the 5 members. The best model per row is in bold. Full tables (RMSE, pixel level, single members, training) are in `docs/results_lstm.md`; the splits are described in `docs/splits.md`.

## Models

| model | run | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| Mean | `mean` | Baseline: every test pixel gets the mean yield of the training pixels. |
| Mean crop×country | `mean_crop_country` | Baseline: the training mean yield of the pixel's crop × country. |
| LSTM | `lstm_s2` | The paper's pixel LSTM: 12 Sentinel-2 bands × 24 time steps, 2 LSTM layers (128) + FC head, one model, early stopping on val MSE. |
| LSTM + ADM | `lstm_s2_adm` | Same LSTM with the paper's ADM input fusion: + 4 weather bands per time step, + soil (48) and topography (5) repeated at every time step. |
| LSTM v2 | `lstm_v2_s2` | LSTM with NDVI/NDRE/NDMI, an observed-time-step channel, one-hot crop and country, yield z-scored per crop × country, max. 500 pixels per field and epoch, dropout + weight decay, and a 5-model ensemble. |
| Patch LSTM | `lstm_patch_s2` | LSTM v2, but each pixel is seen with its 5 × 5 neighbourhood: a small CNN per time step, then the LSTM. 5-model ensemble. |
| Patch LSTM v3 | `lstm_patch_v3_s2` | Patch LSTM + training augmentation against season shifts: the time series randomly shifted by ±1 step, observed steps dropped with p = 0.1. 5-model ensemble. |
| Patch LSTM v3 (10-day) | `lstm_patch_v3_w10_s2` | Patch LSTM v3 on a denser Sentinel-2 time series rebuilt from the raw scenes: 51 steps of 10 days (best scene per window) instead of 24 of 30 days, time shift ±3 steps (= 30 days). Pooled all-crop splits only. 5-model ensemble. |
| ConvLSTM (10-day) | `convlstm_s2` | Convolutional LSTM on the 5 × 5 neighbourhood: the LSTM gates are 3 × 3 convolutions, so space and time are mixed in one layer (1 layer, 32 channels); on the 10-day Sentinel-2 series, otherwise inputs, training and augmentation as Patch LSTM v3. Pooled all-crop splits only. 5-model ensemble. |

Tried and not kept: a field-image model (temporal encoder + U-Net over the whole field; not better than the patch LSTM on 4 splits), and adding weather, soil + topography or spectral noise to the patch LSTM (no gain on val in a 2-member screening). Time augmentation was the only change that helped on val (median subset field R² 0.33 vs 0.08), so it became Patch LSTM v3. ConvLSTM variants (9 × 9 patch, 64 channels, 2 layers) and 15/30-day time series were screened on val and not kept (`docs/convlstm_screening.md`).

## Notes

- Patch LSTM v3 has the best median subset R² overall (0.31), mainly from LORO; under LOYO it is level with LSTM v2 and the paper. The gain over Patch LSTM is not uniform, and many test subsets have few fields (see `test fields`), so differences below ~0.05 are noise.
- Negative R² means worse than predicting the test set's own mean yield. Often it is a level problem: the held-out year or region is predicted too high or too low overall while the fields are still ranked sensibly (e.g. Brazilian wheat under LOYO). German wheat in its single-crop LOYO split is a real failure (fields not ranked either).
- On whole multi-crop splits the crop × country mean is already a strong baseline (Germany: best model), because most of the variance is between crops; this is why the per country × crop tables are the ones to compare.
- Adding weather, soil and topography (LSTM + ADM, and the screened Patch LSTM variant) did not help.
- ConvLSTM: on the same 10-day series it is level with Patch LSTM v3 (higher in 16 of 36 pooled subsets, median difference 0.00); the model itself brings no gain. The 10-day series helps the patch LSTM a little (median +0.04, 20 of 36 subsets) and lifts the whole-split R² on 5 of 6 splits, but unevenly: clear gains in South America LORO (median subset 0.45 → 0.72) and Europe LOYO, losses in World LOYO (Brazilian soybean and wheat, German rapeseed). Medians over 36 subsets differ by a few hundredths, within the noise of single held-out sets.
- The pooled-split tables mix scopes with very different subset sizes; the Swiss subsets have 2–10 test fields, and Swiss `winter_cereal` is barley and triticale together (per the dataset README).
- The single-crop `world/*` splits and the pre-Switzerland `europe`/`world` runs are not regenerated (old suite, `runs/_old_suite/`); the ConvLSTM and 10-day models exist on the pooled splits only. Full tables: `docs/results_convlstm.md`.
