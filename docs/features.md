# Input features

Generated by `scripts/features_doc.py` from the registry `src/features.py` and the data. Load the features with `src/loader.py`:

```python
from src.loader import load, fit_scaler, YieldDataset
d = load("world/loyo/all", role="train", grid="10d",
         features=["s2", "indices", "observed", "weather", "terrain", "soil:0-30cm", "crop", "country"])
ds = YieldDataset(d, fit_scaler(d))   # torch Dataset, z-scored, NaN -> 0
```

Feature specs are `group` or `group:argument` (e.g. `s2:B04,B08`, `weather:tmean_c,prec_mm`, `weather:all`, `terrain:slope`, `soil:clay,soc@0-60cm`). `d.dyn` is (pixels, steps, features), `d.static` (pixels, features), both in the units below, NaN = missing.

## Time grids

`30d`, `15d`, `10d`: windows of 30 / 15 / 10 days from day 210 to day 720, day 0 = 1 January of the year before the harvest year (`src/season.py`); `d.days` holds the first day of every window. The delivered 24-step YieldSAT cube has no data before day 210; our 30-day grid equals its steps 7–23 except 410 Brazilian pixels where the delivered cube keeps SCL-0 pixels.

| grid | steps | windows with a clear observation (all pixels and steps) |
|---|---:|---:|
| `30d` | 17 | 38% |
| `15d` | 34 | 33% |
| `10d` | 51 | 29% |

Coverage (`present`) = share of values that are not NaN in 200 random pixels of every field-year; for dynamic features over all steps of the 10-day grid, so it includes windows outside the season. Medians are not shown for Switzerland (Swiss values are not inspected).

## `indices` (per time step)

- **What:** Normalised differences (a - b) / (a + b): NDVI = (B08 - B04), NDRE = (B08 - B05), NDMI = (B08 - B11); clipped to [-1, 1].
- **Source:** computed by the loader from the reflectances
- **Known issues:** NaN where the S2 bands are NaN.

## `weather` (per time step)

- **What:** Daily ERA5 aggregated over the same calendar windows as S2: means of temperature, sums of precipitation and GDD (sum of max(0, Tmean - Tbase), Tbase 0 °C for wheat / winter cereal / rapeseed, 10 °C for corn / soybean), cumulative GDD / precipitation since sowing at the window end, frost days (Tmin < 0 °C), heat days (Tmax > 30 °C). One value per field, the same for all its pixels.
- **Source:** `<work>/lstm/data_w<days>/weather.npy`, `scripts/LSTM/13_build_weather_grid.py` from the daily ERA5 CSVs
- **Known issues:** NaN outside the CSV record (sowing - 60 d .. harvest + 60 d); cumulative values NaN before sowing. rh_mean, srad, vpd_kpa are missing for all Brazilian and most Argentine fields (not in the default set). Replaces the delivered weather bands, which were sums between selected scenes in K and m (mostly the gap length).

## `terrain` (per pixel)

- **What:** Elevation from the raw SRTM DEM; slope, aspect and curvature from a Gaussian-weighted local quadratic fit (σ 1.5 px ≈ 30 m) per pixel; rel_elevation = elevation minus the field median.
- **Source:** `<work>/lstm/terrain.npy`, `scripts/LSTM/14_build_terrain.py` from the raw `dem/dem-*.tif`
- **Known issues:** Replaces the delivered slope (unknown unit), curvature (clipped, integers in Brazil) and TWI (missing for most pixels; not recomputed, needs the upstream catchment). The delivered elevation is 0 for 408 Brazilian pixels; here it is the raw DEM value. The Brazilian DEM is integer metres in 30 m blocks, so its slopes are coarser.

## `soil` (per pixel)

- **What:** SoilGrids 2.0 at 250 m: 8 properties at 6 standard depths, converted from mapped units, averaged over the requested depth weighted by layer thickness (default 0-30 cm).
- **Source:** `<work>/lstm/data/static.npy` (= raw `soil/*.tif`), converted by `src/soil.py`
- **Known issues:** Near-constant within a field (250 m). Uncertainty layers are not offered (unit undocumented, extreme tails).

Depth spec: any range within 0–200 cm, averaged over the standard layers 0–5, 5–15, 15–30, 30–60, 60–100, 100–200 cm weighted by thickness. Shown: 0–30 cm.

## `crop` (per pixel)

- **What:** One-hot crop over all crops in the dataset.
- **Source:** `meta.parquet`
- **Known issues:** Swiss winter_cereal is barley and triticale together (per the dataset README).

## `country` (per pixel)

- **What:** One-hot country over all countries in the dataset.
- **Source:** `meta.parquet`
- **Known issues:** Under LORO a held-out country's column is all zeros in training.
